Heidi Copy Hotkeys

In Heidi, press Ctrl+A then Ctrl+C to copy all text, then:


PageUp → Copies HPI section to clipboard (asterisks removed)


PageDown → Copies A&P section to clipboard (asterisks removed)


Then paste into Cerner PowerChart with Ctrl+V.


Configure hotkeys in Settings → Preferences
