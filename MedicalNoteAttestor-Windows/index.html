Heidi Copy Hotkeys

Use global hotkeys to quickly copy HPI or A&P sections from Heidi to your clipboard.


How to use:

  1. In Heidi, select all text (Ctrl+A) and copy (Ctrl+C)
  2. Press Page Up to copy just the HPI section
  3. Paste (Ctrl+V) into Cerner Powerchart HPI field
  4. Press Page Down to copy just the A&P section
  5. Paste (Ctrl+V) into Cerner A&P field

Note: The hotkeys read from your clipboard and extract just the section you need. Asterisks are automatically stripped.


Change hotkeys: Go to Settings → Preferences
