Exam Dot Phrase

Persists across sessions. Pastes into Cerner Exam field.
How to use (v2):
1. In Heidi, Ctrl+A then Ctrl+C to copy all text
2. Press Capture hotkey — HPI and A/P load into slots
3. Switch to Cerner (one trip):
  • Click HPI field → F9 (auto-pastes)
  • Click Exam field → F10 (auto-pastes)
  • Click A/P field → F11 (auto-pastes + action items)
4. Save draft. Repeat for next patient.
